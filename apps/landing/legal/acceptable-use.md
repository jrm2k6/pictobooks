# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: March 8, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") describes the rules and restrictions that apply when using **Pictobook** ("Pictobook", "we", "our", or "us").

By using the Pictobook website, applications, or services (collectively, the **Service**), you agree to comply with this Policy.

Failure to follow these rules may result in suspension or termination of your access to the Service.

---

## 2. Permitted Use

Pictobook is designed to allow users to upload photographs and generate creative materials such as coloring books, tracing pages, or other printable artwork.

You may use the Service only for lawful purposes and in accordance with this Policy.

---

## 3. Prohibited Content

You agree not to upload, generate, distribute, or otherwise use the Service to create or process content that:

### Violates Intellectual Property Rights

Content that infringes the rights of others, including:

* copyrighted images without permission
* trademarked characters or brand assets
* images protected by intellectual property laws

Examples may include copyrighted characters from movies, television, or games when you do not have permission to use them.

---

### Is Illegal

Content that violates any applicable law or regulation.

This includes, but is not limited to:

* fraud
* harassment
* unlawful distribution of copyrighted materials

---

### Exploits or Endangers Minors

Content involving the abuse, exploitation, or endangerment of minors is strictly prohibited.

Any such material may be reported to appropriate authorities.

---

### Contains Hate or Harassment

Content that promotes:

* hate speech
* harassment
* discrimination
* threats or violence against individuals or groups

---

### Contains Explicit or Adult Content

Sexually explicit or pornographic content is not permitted on the platform.

---

### Contains Malicious Code

You may not upload files or content that contain:

* malware
* viruses
* malicious scripts
* attempts to exploit system vulnerabilities

---

## 4. Abuse of the Service

You may not use the Service to:

* attempt to reverse engineer the platform
* scrape or extract large amounts of data
* use automated bots without permission
* overload or disrupt the Service infrastructure
* circumvent security or access controls

---

## 5. Enforcement

Pictobook reserves the right to take action against violations of this Policy.

Actions may include:

* removing offending content
* suspending or terminating accounts
* restricting access to the Service
* reporting illegal activities to authorities
