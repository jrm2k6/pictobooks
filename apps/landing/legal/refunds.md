# Refund and Fulfillment Policy

**Last updated: March 8, 2026**

## 1. Overview

This Refund and Fulfillment Policy explains the terms under which purchases made through **Pictobook** ("Pictobook", "we", "our", or "us") may be refunded, replaced, or cancelled.

By purchasing products or services through Pictobook, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

---

## 2. Digital Products

Digital products may include downloadable coloring pages, tracing pages, or other digital files generated through the Service.

Because digital products are delivered immediately and cannot be returned, **digital purchases are generally non-refundable once delivered**, except in cases where:

* the file cannot be accessed
* the file is corrupted
* the product was not delivered

If you experience issues accessing your digital purchase, please contact us and we will attempt to resolve the issue.

---

## 3. Physical Products

If Pictobook offers printed coloring books or other physical goods, refunds or replacements may be available in the following situations:

* the product arrives **damaged**
* the product is **defective**
* the **wrong item** was delivered
* the product **fails to arrive**

Requests for refunds or replacements must typically be made within **14 days of delivery**.

You may be asked to provide:

* photos of the damaged or incorrect product
* your order number
* a description of the issue

---

## 4. Order Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled **before production begins**.

Because many products are generated and printed on demand, cancellations may not be possible once production has started.

---

## 5. Shipping

Shipping times are estimates and may vary depending on:

* shipping carriers
* destination
* production time
* external factors outside our control

Pictobook is not responsible for delays caused by shipping providers or customs processing.

---

## 6. Lost or Stolen Packages

If a package is marked as delivered but cannot be located, please contact us.

We will work with the shipping carrier to investigate the issue. Resolution may vary depending on the circumstances.

---

## 7. Refund Method

Approved refunds will generally be issued using the **original payment method** used during purchase.

Refund processing times may depend on your payment provider.

---

## 8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
